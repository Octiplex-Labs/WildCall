WildCall blocks or identifies whole families of phone numbers, not just numbers one at a time.

ONE RULE, UP TO A MILLION NUMBERS
End a number with * to cover every number that starts the same way. In France, 0162* refuses every call from the 01 62 range. For each rule you choose to block the call or to identify it: an identified call still rings, with the label you picked on screen.

RANGES PUBLISHED BY REGULATORS
WildCall ships packs built from public data:
• France: blocks allocated in the ranges reserved for telemarketing (ARCEP), mainland and overseas
• Spain: the 400 range for commercial calls
• Peru: the 500 series for commercial calls
• Germany: numbers the Bundesnetzagentur acted against
• United States: numbers reported to the FTC and the FCC
Only your country's packs are enabled at first. You turn the others on or off in Settings. Packs update from Octiplex's public catalogue, and every pack is signed.

NO DATA COLLECTED
Your rules stay on your iPhone. WildCall sees neither your calls nor your contacts: the app hands a list of numbers to iOS, and iOS does the filtering. The only network access is downloading packs. iOS never filters numbers that are in your contacts.

GOOD TO KNOW
• After installing, turn on the 4 WildCall lines in Settings > Apps > Phone > Call Blocking & Identification. iOS limits each extension to fewer than 2 million numbers, so WildCall uses four.
• You can export your rules to a JSON file.
• The app is free and has no ads. An optional tip is offered in Settings.
• The source code is public: github.com/Octiplex-Labs/WildCall
